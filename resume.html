Summary

Senior software engineer, five years in PHP and JavaScript — Laravel backends, React and Next.js frontends, and the Linux infrastructure they run on.

I currently maintain the multi-country company-registry platforms at Companies House Insights: order and payment flows, notification pipelines, queued and scheduled processing, and MySQL, across markets that share one architecture.

I'm comfortable owning a feature from design through schema and migration work to deployment on Linux servers — and I'd rather make a system boring and reliable than clever and fragile.
