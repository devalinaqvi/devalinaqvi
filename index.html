01

About

I started out in 2020 at a web studio in Multan, writing PHP for dynamic sites and contributing inventory and workflow modules to an ERP used by business clients. Agency work came next — building REST APIs in Laravel, Nest.js, and Express.js, integrating CRM and marketing-automation platforms, and keeping legacy CodeIgniter and Kohana systems healthy while they carried real traffic.

Today I'm a senior engineer at Companies House Insights, maintaining the Laravel platforms behind company-registry data products across Singapore, Indonesia, Thailand, the Philippines, Malaysia, and other markets. The platforms share a common architecture, so a change is rarely for one country — it has to hold up everywhere.

I like owning a feature end to end: shaping the design, writing the migration, shipping the code, and watching it behave in production. I'd rather make a system boring and reliable than clever and fragile, and most of that comes down to reading code carefully, changing it conservatively, and leaving it easier to maintain than I found it.
